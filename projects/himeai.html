Overview

HimeAI is a personal assistant that lives on my own computer. You chat with Hime in a web window, and she answers with text, expressions and gestures that move her avatar. Nothing is sent to the cloud.

I started it to learn how language models really work, so I am also building my own small model from scratch alongside ready-made local models.

What I built

  1. A FastAPI backend

    REST and WebSocket endpoints, a settings file, an emergency stop and an allow-list for anything that touches the PC.

  2. My own Transformer language model

    Tokenizer, attention, training loop and text generation written by hand, trained on TinyStories, then fine-tuned for chat with a persona.

  3. Local Ollama models

    Switch to a stronger local model from the chat window, with personality presets on top.

  4. Emotions for the avatar

    The model tags its replies with expressions and gestures, which drive Hime's face and pose.

Inside HimeAI

Everything she can do

Eight areas, one companion. Pick one to see what's inside and how it works.

Mind

She thinks with a local model on my laptop GPU, and her mood carries over between chats.

  1. Your words
  2. Thinks first
  3. gemma3 via Ollama
  4. Reply + emotion

  • Local brain

    Any Ollama model (gemma3:4b by default), or my own small Transformer written from scratch.

  • Five personalities

    Himedere by default, plus Warm Princess, Royal Advisor, Spirited Heir and Reserved Noble, with sliders for humor and energy.

  • Thinks before hard answers

    Math, plans, code and comparisons get a private notes pass first. The notes are never stored.

  • A mood that lasts

    Happiness, energy and sulking drift over time and react to how you treat her.

  • Live replies

    Text streams in piece by piece while her voice is still being made.

  • Never robotic

    Fixed lines get reworded each time, but your own words, like reminder text, stay exact.

Voice

Hands-free in English and Japanese, with a voice that follows her emotion.

  1. "Hey, Hime-sama"
  2. faster-whisper
  3. Her reply
  4. Kokoro · VOICEVOX

  • Wake word

    Say "Hey, Hime-sama" or turn voice on. Each phrase is sent after a second of quiet, with live word previews.

  • Hears both languages

    faster-whisper on the CPU, tuned to pick English or Japanese and drop Whisper's made-up phrases.

  • Two voices

    Kokoro-82M for English, VOICEVOX for Japanese. Each sentence goes to the voice of its own language.

  • Emotional Japanese

    VOICEVOX switches style and tunes pitch, speed and intonation to how she feels.

  • Typing in sync

    Replies type out in step with her voice, pausing where she pauses.

  • Real visualizer

    The bars follow her real voice, or the music your PC is playing.

Avatar

A Live2D avatar that reacts, idles and dances. The model is by Snow Bear Project; the behaviour around it is my code.

  1. Emotion tag
  2. Expression + level
  3. Gesture + lip sync
  4. On your desktop

  • 16 emotions

    8 basic and 8 stronger or mixed ones, each with levels, plus gestures and lip sync.

  • Reacts to touch

    Poke her and she complains, pat her head and she purrs, in English or Japanese.

  • An idle life

    She watches your mouse, sways, yawns, hums, and sulks if you ignore her too long.

  • Dances to music

    Finds the beat in what your PC plays and picks calm or hype moves to match.

  • Lives on your desktop

    A transparent, always-on-top window. Clicks pass through everywhere except her.

  • Falls asleep

    When the AI goes offline she dozes off, then wakes up with a greeting.

Memory

She remembers what matters, finds it by meaning, and can study her own chats.

  1. "Remember that…"
  2. Local SQLite
  3. Recall by meaning
  4. Her next reply

  • Remembers on request

    "Remember my cat is called Mochi." Facts stay in a local file you can edit. Passwords are never saved.

  • Recall by meaning

    An embedding model finds the right memory even when you word it differently.

  • Obsidian vault

    Memories mirror to a Markdown vault with a daily log, and she can search your notes and to-dos.

  • Learns in the background

    Files, pictures and apps you use get summarised while she's idle.

  • Retrains herself

    One button fine-tunes her brain (QLoRA) on newer chats, in the background.

Senses

Optional eyes and ears: she can see your screen and webcam, and hear what your PC plays.

  1. Screen · webcam · audio
  2. Privacy checks
  3. A short summary
  4. A reaction

  • Screen awareness

    She glances at your screen when it changes and reacts, like a friend watching along. Banking and private tabs are skipped.

  • Listening along

    Hears the last few seconds your PC played, only when asked, kept in memory only.

  • Webcam senses

    Gestures, feelings and presence, detected in the browser with MediaPipe. Only a summary reaches the backend.

  • Gestures she answers

    A wave, a thumbs up, a peace sign, a heart, or a stretch after a long session.

  • What she sees

    A live page shows her webcam overlay, her last look at the screen, and every step in between.

Everyday help

Small jobs, started only by your own words. Anything that opens something asks first.

  1. Your words
  2. Skill router
  3. Done on your PC
  4. She tells you

  • Time and plans

    Timers, reminders, alarms, a calendar with Google sync, weather and a morning briefing.

  • Notes and math

    Quick notes, and an exact calculator that understands words, percent and Japanese.

  • Looks things up

    Wikipedia facts, and web research that keeps short notes on the pages she read.

  • Media control

    Spotify, YouTube, VLC and per-app volume by voice, plus a playlist for your mood.

    Reads documents in folders you pick and drafts a resume or letter as a Word file.

  • Art credit finder

    Finds the artist behind uncredited art from a username or the picture, using public sources only.

Discord

She chats, moderates and plays music in my Discord server.

  1. Server message
  2. Rules + strikes
  3. Asks me first
  4. Action

  • Chat bot

    The full assistant in my DMs. Everyone else gets a sandboxed guest chat with no memory or files.

  • Moderator

    Spam, scam links and raids checked against rules, with a strike ladder from warning to timeout.

  • Anti-nuke

    Mass deletes or bans strip the culprit's roles at once, and everything can be restored in one click.

  • Voice channel DJ

    Joins a call, plays music or radio, and speaks her replies over it.

  • Mod dashboard

    Every case, waiting decision and live check is visible on one page.

  • Messages

    Reads Gmail and WhatsApp notifications. She always asks before sending anything.

Safety

Every power is off by default, checked on the server, and asks before it acts.

  1. A request
  2. Per-start token
  3. Switch checked
  4. She asks you

  • Locked to my PC

    Only the HimeAI page can reach the backend, with a secret token that's new on every start.

  • A switch for every power

    Apps, search, microphone, webcam, screen and files each have their own switch, off by default.

  • The Shield

    Defender and firewall status, link checks, leaked-password checks and file lookups by hash.

  • Defend mode

    One switch locks down her senses and outside access.

  • Guest mode

    When a friend is around, her memory, files and history stay hidden.

  • The Brain

    A monitor page for services, CPU and GPU, mood, memory, permissions and logs.

    Why I built her

    HimeAI started as a way to learn how language models really work. Next to ready-made local models, I wrote my own small Transformer from scratch: tokenizer, attention, training loop and text generation, trained on TinyStories and fine-tuned for chat with a persona.

    It grew into a full companion. Everything runs on my laptop (an RTX 4050 with 6 GB), and nothing leaves it unless I switch a connection on.

HimeAI

A local desktop AI companion that understands speech, remembers conversations, controls the computer, and helps with everyday tasks, personified by the HimeAI VTuber mascot.

RoleSolo developer
Year2026
StackPython · FastAPI · PyTorch
StatusIn development

Overview

HimeAI is a personal assistant that runs locally on my own PC. A FastAPI backend serves a web chat interface over REST and WebSocket, and Hime replies with text, expressions and gestures that drive her avatar.

Description

I am building my own small Transformer language model from scratch as a learning project, trained on TinyStories and fine-tuned for chat, alongside selectable Ollama models for everyday use.

  • Own Transformer LLM: tokenizer, attention, training loop and inference written by hand
  • Ollama model option with personality presets
  • Emotion and gesture tags that animate the avatar
  • Allow-listed PC control with an emergency stop
  • Planned: Japanese and English voice, speech input, long-term memory
